# Management questions & analytical rules

1. How did sales, profit and margin change year on year? Compare like-for-like months.
2. Which categories and sub-categories have high sales but low/negative profit? Prioritize profit dollars, not just margin.
3. How does profit vary across regions and customer segments?
4. Does discounting co-occur with losses? Segment by product categories and region; association is not causation.
5. Where are the top and bottom 10 sub-categories/products? Be careful with low-volume items.
6. Which interventions could management *test* rather than assume work? Examples: discount-policy pilots or shipping-cost audits.

Avoid invented revenue targets; if the dataset has none, do not label a KPI as against target. State the time period and fiscal/calendar convention.
